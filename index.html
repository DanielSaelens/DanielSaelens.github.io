About me

I'm a Computer Science graduate of Colorado School of Mines (December 2026), with a focus in Business Economics. I've spent 12 years in the Army National Guard, where I'm a Staff Sergeant (SSG) serving as Section Chief and Network Operations Supervisor, leading a 10-person team that keeps email, phone, and network services running across sites in three countries. Some of my proudest work there isn't technical: I've mentored six soldiers through their education benefits, and all six are now pursuing college degrees.

That mix shapes how I build software: practical, reliable, and made with the people using it in mind. At Mines I managed the software team on a capstone that became a production tool for HP engineers, and I've built everything from Android apps in Kotlin to compilers in Scala and systems code in C++. I'm also an Eagle Scout.
